## Love App 24 — The Greek Way
A poetic Valentine ritual: 24 hearts, 24 hours of love.

### Description
Love App 24 — The Greek Way is a refined Valentine’s Day ritual. On February 14, **24 hearts unlock gradually—one per hour**—revealing love quotes from the Greek tradition, displayed in three lines (**Ancient Greek, Modern Greek, English**). The spiral layout, gentle animations, and thoughtful typography create a uniquely poetic experience.

### Purpose
Transform Valentine’s Day into a serene, beautiful 24-hour ritual that connects love with the wisdom of Greek literature and philosophy.

### Features
- **24 hearts unlocking hourly on 14/2**
- **Love quotes** in Ancient Greek, Modern Greek, and English
- **Elegant spiral layout** with subtle effects
- **Reminder notifications** (active only on 14/2)
- **Quiet Hours** to mute notifications when desired
- **Daily check‑in** (mood score + optional note, stored on device)
- **Days Together (Μετρητής Σχέσης)**: pick your relationship start date and see years/months/days together (+ total days)
- **Love Quiz (Κουίζ για Ζευγάρια)**: 10 questions (A/B/C) with partner answer setup and final score (stored on device)
- **Sign in with Google or Apple — or continue as guest**
- **Refined typography and polished aesthetics**
- **Unlocked content remains available on your device for a few days after 14/2**
